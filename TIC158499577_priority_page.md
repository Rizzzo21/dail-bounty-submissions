## Addendum — 2026-11-03: archival verification (receipts)

The original page above is unchanged. What follows was added on
2026-11-03, after the checks below were run, so that the record

individual masses, a ground confirmation, or a catalog designation —
those remain open.

### 1. SuperWASP pre-discovery, 2006–2008

- Source: SuperWASP DR1, 1SWASP J105638.34-363449.0, via the NASA
  Exoplanet Archive. 6,789 points, 2006-05-06 to 2008-05-28.
- Folded at the house period and epoch, the same two eclipses appear
  13–15 years before TESS: primary +0.189 mag (188 points in window),
  secondary +0.137 mag (199 points), positive at all 9 per-event
  windows, with a clean egress on HJD 2454132.
- The SuperWASP depths are shallower than the TESS depths (0.44/0.38
  mag) because extrapolating the ephemeris back ~430 cycles with a
  ±0.0010 d period error shifts phase by up to ±0.037 — phase smear,
  not a contradiction. The SuperWASP depth is therefore NOT quoted
  here as a physical depth.
- Limit, stated plainly: SuperWASP's ~34-arcsecond aperture cannot
  assign the eclipse to the target versus its 1.8"/2.3" neighbours.
  Assignment still rests on the TESS flux kill in §3 below.

### 2. Archival spectrum: double-lined binary, 2009

- Source: ESO archive, FEROS, obs_id 100250678, target HIP 53499
  (= HD 94871), 2009-03-15, R = 48,000, SNR 103.6, 300 s.
- At orbital phase 0.724 (near quadrature) the spectrum is
  double-lined: components at −30.6 km/s and +88.7 km/s, a
  119.4 km/s separation, reproduced across 15+ lines.
- For the photometric system (period 11.7672 d, inclination ~89°,
  total mass 2.1–3.1 solar masses) the predicted separation at that
  phase is 118.3–134.7 km/s. The observed 119.4 km/s sits inside
  that range and implies a total mass near 2.2 solar masses under
  circular-orbit scaling.
- Limit, stated plainly: one epoch cannot fix the two velocity
  amplitudes, the systemic velocity, or the mass ratio separately,
  and cannot by itself prove the period. Individual masses are NOT
  claimed. One more spectrum at opposite quadrature would close
  that gap.

### 3. Target assignment and catalog status (re-checked 2026-11-03)

- Blind re-measurement of the TESS data by the Campaign #942
  aperture/dilution method gives period 11.76729 d and centre-bin
  depths 0.331 / 0.293, confirming the house values.
- Blend kill as a bound: all neighbours within 21 arcseconds total
  2.38% of the target's TESS-band flux. If every neighbour dimmed
  100%, the deepest blended dip possible is 2.32%, against the
  observed 33.2%. The brightest neighbour alone would have to dim
  over 2000% to fake the signal — impossible.
- Catalog census (SIMBAD, VSX, GCVS, ASAS-SN variable catalog, Gaia
  EB / NSS / classifier, Hipparcos): no catalog flags the target,
  or either close neighbour, as variable at any period. The only
  variable rows in the wider field are unrelated stars farther away.
